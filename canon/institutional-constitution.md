# Institutional Constitution — Tattvashila

**Document Type:** Constitutional Structure — Institutional Architecture Governance
**Canonical Status:** Primary — Supreme (governs institutional structure)
**Scope:** The whole of Tattvashila — every layer, section, wing, route, document, and future function
**Status:** **Constitutional Structure Locked v1.0**
**Purpose:** To define, permanently and authoritatively, what Tattvashila is, how it is
structured, what each layer and section is responsible for, how the website relates to
Traksha, and the rules under which the institution may expand.

---

## 0. Authority and Precedence

This document is the **single source of truth for the institutional structure of
Tattvashila**. All future development, content, navigation, documentation, and
implementation decisions must conform to the structure defined here.

Precedence is divided by domain, so that this document and the existing canon do not
compete:

- On questions of **institutional structure** — what the institution is, its layers, its
  wings, where a section belongs, what each part is responsible for, how the website and
  Traksha relate — **this Constitution governs.** It is supreme.
- On questions of **manuscript composition, canonical language, rendering fidelity, and
  preservation** — the existing `canon/` documents govern (Canonical Lexicon, Rendering
  Constitution, Granthālaya Preservation Principles, Doctrinal Continuity Governance, and
  the manuscript-integrity and rendering documents).
- On the content of a **manuscript itself**, the manuscript is the final authority, as the
  canon already establishes.

Where any existing document, code description, or content string contradicts the structure
defined here, **this Constitution prevails**, and the conflicting reference is to be
reconciled to it (see Section 9).

## 2. Core Definitions

These definitions are binding. They extend — and must remain consistent with — the
Canonical Lexicon entries for *Tattvashila*, *Saṃsthā*, *Granthālaya*, and *Grantha*.

**Tattvashila is the Institution.**
A Saṃsthā — an enduring philosophical body defined by what it composes, preserves, and
holds as its positions. It is not a personal brand, a product, a startup, a platform, an
NGO, or "a body of work." The name means *the foundation stone*: the slow, patient laying
of a philosophical foundation.

**Traksha is NOT a product.**
Traksha is **not** a standalone business unit. Traksha is the **institutional technology
backbone and implementation layer**. All future practical systems of the institution
operate *through* Traksha. The practical functions of the institution — TRK Identity,
Tattvapeetha, Raksha, Shreetattva, Vanatattva, Volunteer Systems, TattvaCoin, and future
institutional functions — are functions delivered through the Traksha layer, not
independent ventures.

**Granthālaya is an institutional manuscript library.**
It is **not** the identity of Tattvashila and **not** the institution itself. It is **one
part of the Knowledge & Record layer** — the abode in which completed manuscripts reside
permanently. (Its internal preservation rules and manuscript hierarchy remain governed by
the canon.)

**Philosophy contains institutional doctrine** — the working ideas and settled positions
of the institution.

**Origin contains institutional provenance and the founding narrative** — why the
institution exists and what it was built in response to.

**Notes contain short-form reflections** — rare, irregular writing in the institution's
public voice.

**Archive contains the institutional record** — the complete, slowly-growing record of
what has reached readiness.

---

## 3. Responsibility of Each Layer

**Foundation** — establishes *who the institution is and why*. It holds identity, doctrine,
and provenance. It is the layer a reader consults to understand what Tattvashila believes
and where it came from. Foundation does not host practical systems and does not hold
published output; it holds belief and origin.

**Knowledge & Record** — holds *what the institution has thought, written, and preserved*.
It is the layer of composed manuscripts (Granthālaya), short-form reflections (Notes), and
the institutional record (Archive). It is memory and output, held to a standard of
readiness.

**Traksha** — delivers *what the institution does in practice*. It is the technology
backbone and implementation layer through which every practical function operates. No
practical system stands outside Traksha; new functions are added *within* it.

## 5. Relationship Between the Website and Traksha

The **website is the canonical institutional environment** — there is exactly **one**
canonical institutional website, and the institution must not be fragmented into multiple
web properties. (This restates and is bound by the standing institutional direction to keep
a single canonical site and integrate all systems into it.)

- The **website** is the public-facing surface of the institution. It expresses the
  Foundation (Philosophy, Origin) and the Knowledge & Record (Granthālaya, Notes, Archive).
- **Traksha** is the institutional technology backbone and implementation layer. Practical
  systems are delivered *through* Traksha and surfaced *within* the single canonical
  website — never as separate sites, parallel environments, or standalone properties.
- Manuscript rendering and calibration systems attach **only to Granthālaya routes** and
  live inside the existing institutional architecture.
- When a practical function (e.g. a Traksha function) becomes web-facing, it appears inside
  the one canonical website, under the institution's existing navigation and continuity —
  never as a fork of the institution.

In short: **the website is how the institution is read; Traksha is how the institution
acts. Both belong to one institution and one canonical site.**

---

## 6. Future Expansion Rules

1. **The three-layer shape is fixed.** Every new thing the institution builds is a section
   *within* Foundation, Knowledge & Record, or Traksha. Nothing is added as a fourth
   top-level layer except by formal amendment (Section 10).
2. **Practical systems belong to Traksha.** Any new practical/technological function is
   added under Traksha as a Future Institutional Function. It is never created as a separate
   product, business unit, or website.
3. **Belief belongs to Foundation; output belongs to Knowledge & Record.** New doctrinal or
   provenance material extends Philosophy or Origin. New composed/published material extends
   Granthālaya, Notes, or Archive according to its kind.
4. **One canonical website.** No new web property is created for any function. Everything
   integrates into the single canonical institutional site.
5. **Granthālaya stays one wing.** Granthālaya must never be re-elevated to stand for the
   institution as a whole. It remains one section of Knowledge & Record.
6. **Naming and definitions are protected.** New institutional terms are added through the
   Canonical Lexicon's governance; their *structural placement* is governed here.
7. **Readiness over schedule.** Sections are revealed when ready, consistent with the
   institution's standing disposition to hold until complete.

## 7. Visitor Comprehension Standard

The institution's surfaces should make the structure legible:

- **Quickly:** Tattvashila is an institution for grounded, conscious living — organized into
  a Foundation, a body of Knowledge & Record, and the Traksha implementation layer.
- **With time:** *what it believes* (Philosophy), *why it exists* (Origin), *what it has
  written and preserved* (Granthālaya, Notes, Archive), and *how it acts in practice*
  (Traksha and its functions).
- **In depth:** the doctrine, the founding narrative, and the manuscripts themselves —
  read in the Granthālaya — with every practical function understood as operating through
  Traksha inside one canonical institution.

---

## 8. What This Constitution Does Not Govern

This document does not govern manuscript content, canonical language, typography, rendering
fidelity, or preservation mechanics. Those remain with the canon. This document governs
*structure*: what exists, where it belongs, and what it is responsible for.

## 10. Amendment Process

This structure is locked at **v1.0**. It may be amended only by:

1. An explicit, documented institutional decision to change the structure;
2. A new version of this document (e.g. v1.1, v2.0) that states what changed and why;
3. Reconciliation of every affected reference to the new version.

Stylistic edits, readability passes, or implementation convenience are **not** grounds for
amendment. Until amended, v1.0 is binding.
