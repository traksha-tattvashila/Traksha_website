# Institutional Constitution — Tattvashila

**Document Type:** Constitutional Structure — Institutional Architecture Governance
**Canonical Status:** Primary — Supreme (governs institutional structure)
**Scope:** The whole of Tattvashila — every layer, section, wing, route, document, and future function
**Status:** **Constitutional Architecture Fully Frozen v1.0**
**Purpose:** To define, permanently and authoritatively, what Tattvashila is, how it is structured, what each layer and section is responsible for, how the homepage relates to the institution, how Traksha is exposed, and the rules under which the institution may expand.

---

## 0. Authority and Precedence

This document is the **single authoritative source of truth for the institutional structure of Tattvashila**. All future development, content, navigation, documentation, and implementation decisions must conform to the structure defined here.

Precedence is divided by domain, so that this document and the existing canon do not compete:

- On questions of **institutional structure** — what the institution is, its layers, its wings, where a section belongs, what each part is responsible for, how the homepage and Traksha relate, and how the institution expands — **this Constitution governs**.
- On questions of **manuscript composition, canonical language, rendering fidelity, and preservation** — the existing `canon/` documents govern.
- On the content of a **manuscript itself**, the manuscript is the final authority, as the canon already establishes.

Where any existing document, code description, content string, route, navigation label, or implementation detail contradicts the structure defined here, **this Constitution prevails**, and the conflicting reference must be reconciled to it in a separately sanctioned implementation or documentation task.

No new architecture, institutional layer, terminology, route, page, or structural category may be introduced outside this Constitution except by formal amendment.

## 2. Locked Definitions

These definitions are binding.

**Tattvashila = Institution.**
Tattvashila is the institution as a whole. It is not a product, startup, platform, personal brand, or homepage identity. All layers, sections, records, and future functions belong to Tattvashila.

**Foundation = the institutional foundation layer.**
Foundation contains **Philosophy** and **Origin**. It holds institutional doctrine and institutional provenance.

**Philosophy = institutional doctrine.**
Philosophy contains what the institution believes, its working ideas, and its settled positions.

**Origin = institutional provenance.**
Origin contains why the institution exists and the founding narrative from which it emerged.

**Knowledge & Record = the institutional knowledge and memory layer.**
Knowledge & Record contains **Granthalaya**, **Notes**, and **Archive**. It holds what the institution has composed, considered, preserved, and made ready.

**Granthalaya = institutional manuscript library.**
Granthalaya is not homepage identity and is not the institution itself. It is one section within Knowledge & Record.

**Notes = short-form reflective record.**
Notes belong to Knowledge & Record. They are not a casual blog layer and are not outside the institutional record family.

**Archive = institutional record.**
Archive belongs to Knowledge & Record. It preserves the institutional record of what has reached readiness.

**Traksha = institutional technology backbone.**
Traksha is not a product, standalone venture, startup vertical, or separate institution. Traksha is the institutional technology backbone through which practical institutional systems are built and operated.

**Future Institutional Functions = future functions under Traksha.**
Future practical systems must be placed under Traksha unless a formal constitutional amendment changes the structure.

## 3. Layer Experience Rules

### 3.1 Foundation Experience Rule

Foundation must remain **modern + timeless**.

Foundation must not become ancient recreation, nostalgic styling, startup marketing, product positioning, or campaign language. Its experience should support doctrine and origin with clarity, restraint, and institutional seriousness.

This applies equally to:

- Philosophy
- Origin

Philosophy and Origin may differ in content and purpose, but both must remain part of the same Foundation layer.

### 3.2 Knowledge & Record Experience Rule

Knowledge & Record is a single experiential family within Tattvashila.

This applies equally to:

- Granthalaya
- Notes
- Archive

These sections may differ in:

- content
- purpose
- format
- information architecture

However, they must share:

- sacred atmosphere
- contemplative pacing
- knowledge preservation tone
- institutional memory function

Granthalaya is the institutional manuscript library, but it does not define the entire Knowledge & Record experience by itself.

Notes and Archive are not secondary surfaces, blog-like extensions, or casual content areas. They are part of the same Knowledge & Record family and must maintain the same institutional seriousness.

Future design, writing, navigation, and implementation decisions for Granthalaya, Notes, and Archive must preserve this shared experiential identity.

### 3.3 Traksha Experience Rule

Traksha must remain a **modern institutional technology experience**.

Traksha must not be presented as a product, consumer app, startup brand, SaaS offering, separate venture, or marketing ecosystem. Its experience should communicate institutional technology, infrastructure, function, continuity, and practical implementation.

This applies to:

- TRK Identity
- Tattvapeetha
- Raksha
- Shreetattva
- Vanatattva
- Volunteer Systems
- TattvaCoin
- Future Institutional Functions

## 4. Responsibility of Each Layer

**Foundation** establishes who the institution is and why it exists. It holds identity, doctrine, and provenance.

**Knowledge & Record** holds what the institution has thought, written, considered, preserved, and made ready. It is the institutional memory and record layer.

**Traksha** delivers what the institution does in practice through technology. It is the backbone through which practical institutional functions operate.

## 6. Homepage Responsibility

The homepage represents **Tattvashila as an institution as a whole**.

The homepage may answer only:

- What is Tattvashila?
- Why does it exist?
- What does it believe?
- What knowledge exists?
- What is Traksha?
- What is being built?
- Where should visitors go next?

The homepage may contain institutional-level previews of:

- Foundation
- Knowledge & Record
- Traksha
- current institutional direction
- next navigation paths

The homepage must remain shallow, orienting, and institutional.

The homepage must not become:

- Philosophy page
- Origin page
- manuscript page
- ecosystem documentation page
- product explanation page
- operational workflow page

Granthalaya must not become homepage identity. Traksha must not become homepage identity. The homepage must represent the institution as a whole.

## 7. Traksha Exposure Model

Homepage contains only a **Traksha preview**.

Homepage may explain:

- what Traksha is
- why it exists
- its role in the institution

Homepage must not contain:

- product-level explanations
- operational workflows

Depth belongs to future Traksha pages.

Future Traksha pages may explain institutional technology systems, identity infrastructure, digital participation systems, operational platforms, and institutional function layers, but only within the constitutional Traksha layer.

## 9. Expansion Governance Rules

All future pages, routes, sections, functions, and documentation surfaces must belong to exactly one constitutional layer:

- Foundation
- Knowledge & Record
- Traksha

The following rules are binding:

1. **No orphan pages.** Every page must have a constitutional home.
2. **No parallel structures.** No new structure may sit beside Foundation, Knowledge & Record, and Traksha.
3. **No duplicate institutional layers.** Existing layer responsibilities must not be recreated under new names.
4. **No fourth top-level layer.** A new top-level layer requires formal constitutional amendment.
5. **Practical systems belong to Traksha.** New practical or technological functions must be placed under Traksha as Future Institutional Functions unless formally amended.
6. **Belief belongs to Foundation.** New doctrine or provenance material extends Philosophy or Origin.
7. **Knowledge output belongs to Knowledge & Record.** New composed, reflective, manuscript, or record material extends Granthalaya, Notes, or Archive according to its kind.
8. **Granthalaya stays one section.** Granthalaya must never be re-elevated to stand for the institution as a whole.
9. **One canonical website.** No new web property is created for any function. Everything integrates into the single canonical institutional site.
10. **Naming and definitions are protected.** New institutional terms require canonical governance; their structural placement is governed here.
11. **Readiness over schedule.** Sections are revealed when ready.

## 10. Relationship Between the Website and Traksha

The **website is the canonical institutional environment**. There is exactly **one** canonical institutional website, and the institution must not be fragmented into multiple web properties.

- The **website** is the public-facing institutional surface.
- **Traksha** is the institutional technology backbone and implementation layer.
- Practical systems are delivered through Traksha and surfaced within the single canonical website.
- When a Traksha function becomes web-facing, it appears inside the one canonical website, under the institution's existing navigation and continuity.
- No Traksha function may become a separate site, parallel environment, standalone product property, or separate venture.

In short: **the website is how the institution is read; Traksha is how the institution acts. Both belong to one institution and one canonical site.**

## 11. Visitor Comprehension Standard

The institution's surfaces should make the structure legible:

- **Quickly:** Tattvashila is an institution organized into Foundation, Knowledge & Record, and Traksha.
- **With time:** visitors can understand what it believes, why it exists, what knowledge it preserves, what Traksha is, and where to go next.
- **In depth:** the full doctrine belongs to Philosophy, the founding narrative belongs to Origin, manuscripts belong to Granthalaya, reflective notes belong to Notes, institutional record belongs to Archive, and practical technology functions belong to Traksha.
